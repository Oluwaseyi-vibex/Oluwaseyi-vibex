## About Me

I am a software engineer and product builder focused on creating practical, high-impact digital products. I enjoy turning ideas into software that people use, from rapid prototypes to production-ready systems.

I’ve built applications across multiple industries and enjoy working across design, product thinking, frontend, backend, infrastructure, and AI.

## What I'm Building

### Lens
AI-powered CFO for SMEs.

Lens helps business owners understand their finances without learning accounting or bookkeeping. By connecting financial accounts and analyzing transactions, Lens delivers clear insights, forecasts, and recommendations through familiar channels like WhatsApp.

### Myle
Lead generation and management platform.

Myle helps businesses discover prospects, identify decision makers, understand customer pain points, and generate personalized outreach at scale.

### Grabit
Own a piece of the internet.

Grabit is a 2D digital land marketplace where businesses and individuals can claim, buy, and compete for ownership of locations on an interactive online map.
